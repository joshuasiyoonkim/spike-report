Three weeks after punching their Champions ticket by running NRG out of a São Paulo final, 100 Thieves stepped onto the Shanghai stage against T1 and didn't blink. 13-8 on Summit, 13-9 on Ascent, 2-0 in the opener, Group A's first result in the books.

## Cryocells Didn't Leave His Form at Home

Summit was the tell. Cryocells closed that map at 289 ACS going 23-9, the kind of stat line that ends a half before it really starts. He kept it going on Ascent too, 277 ACS with a 1.36 rating, and by the time the second map hit its final round T1 were chasing a comeback that never had the legs. This is the same Cryocells who was doing damage in São Paulo, just with a bigger stage and worse jet lag.

## T1's International Debut Started Rough

Ascent actually had a stretch where T1 looked like they belonged, stringing together a few round wins mid-map that made it feel like a series again. It wasn't enough. 100 Thieves answered every push with a cleaner read, and a team playing its first Champions match on an unfamiliar bracket is never going to out-execute a squad that just went through the Americas gauntlet twice in one summer. T1 drop to the lower bracket now, one loss from elimination before the group even wraps up. This wasn't the start they needed.

## Where I Land

I picked 100 Thieves to be the scariest team out of the Americas coming into Shanghai, and an opening 2-0 with Cryocells this locked in isn't exactly talking me out of it. Group A is a long way from over and T1 aren't done. But if this is the version of 100T that shows up every series, the rest of the bracket should be worried.
