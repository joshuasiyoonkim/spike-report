Vitality beat 100 Thieves 3-1 in the lower final, and the scoreline hides how bad the start looked.

## Split looked like the end

100 Thieves took Split 13-5. That's not a close map. At that point I figured the NA run was going to the grand final, and I wasn't alone.

Then Vitality stopped losing rounds they were supposed to win. Summit went 13-10. Ascent went 13-9. Lotus was the real one, 13-11, where Vitality won the last two rounds to take a map that 100T had every chance to close.

Is that a comeback or a map pool problem? Both. 100T were great on the one map they wanted and a step behind on the other three.

## The rematch nobody asked for

Vitality already lost to LOUD in the upper final, 2-1. Now it's the same two teams again on Sunday. Third meeting of the event.

I still think LOUD are the better team on the day, but Vitality earned the right to run it back. They lost a series, dropped a map by eight, and kept coming. Derke has said all he wants is the big trophy he's been chasing for six years. Nobody is closer to it right now.

100 Thieves go home as the last NA team standing. A lower final exit at Champions is still a good result. It just won't feel like one tonight.

The grand final is October 18 at Mercedes-Benz Arena.
