Cloud9 said they weren't leaving, and now they have a roster that backs it up. On November 7 they added alym, Derrek and nightz to FireBallOps and GLYPH. Their post said the trials were done. Five players, built out of 10-player tryout lobbies.

## Who They Got

alym played entry duelist for FURIA this year. nightz joined ENVY mid-season and mostly played controller. Derrek is the veteran here. He played for M80 and QoR in 2026, and before that he was on 100 Thieves and Evil Geniuses.

FireBallOps is the only player left from the 2026 year-end roster. Four new starters is a full reset. OXY already left for Sentinels, so there wasn't much to keep.

## Is It Good Enough?

On paper, it's a team of guys partnered orgs passed on. In a server, that doesn't mean much. I've queued with stacks of five solid players who'd never played together, and the first two weeks look like five people playing five different games. Then somebody takes the calls and it clicks.

That's my worry. Who leads this team? Nobody's said, and a tryout roster without a clear voice falls apart in the first close overtime.

The other reading is that the open path rewards exactly this. Hungry players, no partner money to coast on, and a qualifier where one bad series ends your year. I lean that way. Derrek has seen enough rosters to be the steadying piece, and alym gives them someone who wants to take the first duel.

## What's Next

Kickoff only has four open seats in Americas. The other eight belong to partners. Cloud9's road to one of those seats starts November 20.

I could be wrong about nightz on controller against partner-level teams. But I said I'd rather watch a cut org grind its way back than a partnered one coast. Now they get to prove it.
