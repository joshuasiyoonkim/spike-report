ENVY's new roster is the most interesting team in the NA Kickoff qualifier. exa, Xeppaa, ion, v1c and Paincakes, with Stunner back as head coach. Announced November 21. Two series played. Two series won.

## Why care about a qualifier team?

Because ENVY didn't get a partner slot for 2027. They won Ascension to earn one, and now they're back on the open circuit with a roster that shares zero players with the one that started 2026. Most orgs in that spot quietly walk away. ENVY said they're staying, and this lineup is the proof.

## Is Xeppaa still Xeppaa?

That was my first question. He stepped back from competitive play earlier this year, and this is his first team that isn't Cloud9. Against FlyQuest he answered it: 273 ACS and series MVP in a 2-1 win, including a 15-13 Corrode. That's not rust.

The opener against Yellow Dragon Gaming was a 2-0, 27-17 in rounds. The video up top is a watch party of that series, and ion is the reason to click it. Even the streamer admits mid-match he wasn't familiar with ion's game. He is now.

## The other reading

Two Swiss wins aren't a season. FlyQuest just signed a brand new five of their own, so you could say ENVY beat a team still learning each other's names. I've queued with stacks that win their first two nights together and fall apart the moment someone has a plan for them.

I'm buying it anyway. A flex player with years of franchised reps is the one thing young rosters never have, and Xeppaa is playing like he wants to prove C9 wasn't the ceiling.

The Swiss stage wraps tomorrow. Keep winning and nobody gets to call this a qualifier team for long.
