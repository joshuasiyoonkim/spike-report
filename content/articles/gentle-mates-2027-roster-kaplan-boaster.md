Gentle Mates made it official on November 25: Boaster, Alfajer, Ruxic, MAGNUM and 16-year-old kozzy, with Adam "kaplan" Kaplan as head coach, all pending Riot approval. Everyone's going to talk about the Fnatic reunion. I think the coach is the bigger story.

## The Reunion Is the Easy Part

Boaster and Alfajer won LOCK//IN São Paulo and Masters Tokyo together in 2023. That doesn't vanish because the jersey changed. When I wrote about Alfajer leaving Fnatic, my worry was a star duo with three question marks next to them. Now we know who they are. Ruxic is a proven controller, freed up when NAVI lost its partnership. MAGNUM steps up after a year at Enterprise. kozzy is 16.

Is that enough? On paper, it's thin. In practice, it depends on who's running the room.

## Why kaplan

Sentinels moved on from kaplan in February after a winless Kickoff, and CEO Rob Moore pointed to internal conflict over roster decisions. kaplan wanted changes. The org didn't.

Now he walks into a team that released every player and coach it had. No legacy names to protect, nobody to argue with about who stays. That's the exact opposite of how his Sentinels run ended, and it's why I like this hire more than any of the five players.

## The Risk

kozzy. A 16-year-old duelist in EMEA Kickoff is a huge ask. I've queued with players who were mechanically the best in the lobby and still froze the first time a round actually mattered. You don't find out which kind you've got until January.

The other reading is that Boaster and kaplan are two strong voices fighting over one comms channel. I could be wrong, but a coach with nothing to inherit and an IGL with nothing left to prove sounds like a fit, not a fight.

Gentle Mates' reveal video leans hard into the theatrics. Fair enough. The bench is what I'll be watching.
