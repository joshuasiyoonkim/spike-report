Riot said the quiet part out loud: FUT should have gotten that round back. 100 Thieves still advance. Both of those things are true, and that's the problem.

## What Riot Admitted

After a post-game review, Riot concluded the round 2 pause on Split, when xeus couldn't fire, should have been rolled back. Yesterday the answer on the server was no. Today it's "our bad." The match result stands.

Is that fair? It's honest, and I'll give them that. It's also useless to FUT, who can't replay a series.

## The Desks Did It

The cause is the part that gets me. Riot says repeated mouse interference traced back to the custom metal desks it fabricated for the event. Metal desks, wireless mice, a whole week of pauses. Riot's fix is to ask every player to switch to a wired mouse until the desks get replaced with a different material.

Riot called it the best option available, not an ideal one, and acknowledged that muscle memory matters. Most pros dropped wired mice years ago. Asking them to change a core piece of their setup mid-event, with elimination matches on the schedule, is a real ask.

## Where I Land

There are two readings. One is that Riot handled it fine: found the cause, owned the mistake, fixed the desks. The other is that the fix came a week into the event, after FUT got burned by a ruling they say contradicted what officials had told them in advance.

I'm taking the second one. The wired mouse request is reasonable. The timing isn't. If metal desks were interfering, it should have shown up in testing, not in a 2v1 on Split.

100T are through, and they earned the maps. FUT got an apology.
