Riot dropped the 13.06 preview Monday and buried the real headline under a new game mode. Forget Gauntlet: Glitched for a second. The story is the Warden, the first new rifle to join the 2,900-credit tier since Vandal and Phantom staked that ground out years ago.

## What It Actually Does

50 damage to the body at every range, no falloff, 200 on a headshot, 18 rounds in the mag. Compare that to a Phantom bleeding damage the second a fight goes past mid-range, and the Warden reads like a gun built for players who lose duels to distance, not aim. Fully automatic, with a 2x ADS baked in, chasing that long-range option Riot's been dancing around without committing to since Vandal and Phantom got a sniper cousin that wasn't the Operator.

## Is It Actually a Third Option?

On paper, sure. In practice, I don't buy that anyone's dropping the Vandal for it. Fewer bullets and a lower fire rate mean the Warden loses the spray-transfer fights that decide most rounds in ranked. This is a hold-an-angle gun, not a duel gun. Which is fine, that's a real niche. Operator players who don't want to eat an eco loss over one missed shot finally get a middle ground that isn't just re-buying a Phantom.

## My Actual Worry

Every buy-round read in my Discord already ends with someone guessing what the enemy could be running that we haven't seen yet. Add a third silhouette to hold long angles and site-anchor reads get muddier for at least a week while everyone relearns what a Warden can punish from a window nobody used to respect.

Patch lands September 22. I'm buying one the first pistol round I can afford it, purely to find out if it's actually good or just new.
