It's done. Fnatic announced valyn as its IGL on November 26, a day after making marteen official. A month ago I wrote this up as a report. Now it's real, and the part that bugs me isn't Fnatic. It's that Americas let him go.

## How does this happen?

valyn spent three years at G2 and won four VCT Americas trophies, the last one a best-of-five final in May. G2 parted ways with him on October 27. His reported deal with LYON depended on a partnership slot LYON never got. Eight partner teams, and not one of them had room for a four-time regional champion.

Is that on G2? Partly. They went with kiNgg, and I understand wanting a fresh voice after three years of regional titles that never turned into an international one. But it's a format problem first. Fewer seats means proven players get squeezed out, and the good ones don't wait around for open qualifiers. They leave.

## Does it work in EMEA?

The pieces are clearer now. marteen is signed, and kaajak and Cloud are under contract through 2028. That's a real core for an IGL to shape, not a rebuild from scratch.

The worry is the first month. I've been the one making calls for a stack that came from a different ranked scene, and every default we ran meant something slightly different to each of us. The first week was mostly arguing about what "fast B" meant. A new IGL, a new region and a roster that isn't finished yet is that problem times ten.

I still like it. Fnatic's reveal video is called "We Need An AMERICAN!" and ends with valyn asking if they want to win Champions. They said yes. Two minutes of jokes, but the pitch is clear: the IGL is the signing they built around.

Americas trained him for three years. EMEA gets to find out what he does next.
