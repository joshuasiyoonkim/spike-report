LOUD won Champions a month ago, and the five who did it are already scattering. The one player they reportedly kept is the one whose contract nobody agrees on.

## How did we get here?

tkzin joined LOUD in July on loan from KRÜ SPARK, KRÜ's academy team. No option to buy. THESPIKE Brasil reported LOUD paid US$50,000 for the loan. Then he dropped three aces in his Champions debut against EDG and LOUD beat Vitality 3-1 for the title. That loan aged well.

When it ended this month, LOUD posted a farewell. Riot's reported view is that he's a free agent, because his KRÜ deal was signed for an academy roster that no longer exists. KRÜ disputes that and says he's under contract through the end of 2027. Sheep Esports reports he has a verbal agreement to stay at LOUD anyway. Nothing is official.

## Who's right?

On paper, KRÜ has a point. A contract is a contract. In practice, I side with Riot's reading. If an org dissolves the team you signed for, it shouldn't get to hold the player hostage at the next tier up. KRÜ even says it's open to negotiating a transfer, which tells me they know the ruling isn't going their way.

## Why it matters for LOUD

Look at the rest of that winning five. erde and lukxo are reportedly heading to Leviatán. Darker reportedly has a verbal deal with KRÜ. LOUD is reportedly bringing in cortezia, artzin and Basic. tkzin is the only champion left.

I had a duo last year who carried my whole climb. When he quit, I didn't rebuild around a new five. I just queued with whoever was left, and the games felt like somebody else's account. That's what LOUD is facing if this goes wrong.

They need this ruling more than KRÜ does.
