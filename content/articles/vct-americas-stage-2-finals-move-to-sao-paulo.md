Four teams left. NRG and 100 Thieves play Friday for a straight line into Sunday's grand final. LOUD and G2 play the same day just to keep their season alive. Two Champions Shanghai berths on the table, and none of it happens in LA. For the first time in the region's history, the Americas finals are in São Paulo.

## The Move Actually Matters

Every Stage 2 and Kickoff final I can remember watching happened in the same LA studio with the same seats. Suhai Music Hall this weekend is a different building in a different country, and LOUD gets to play a lower bracket in front of a home crowd for the first time since I've been covering this region. That's not a footnote. A Brazilian org fighting to survive with actual Brazilian fans in the room changes what elimination pressure looks like.

## The Bracket I Called

Back on the 29th I picked NRG and 100 Thieves to meet in the upper final, and here it is with a date attached: Friday, upper final, winner skips straight to Sunday. The loser doesn't go home, they drop into Saturday's lower final against whoever survives G2 and LOUD, the same LOUD that just swept MIBR out two days ago on zero rest. That's the matchup I actually want. Everyone assumes the tired team loses that spot. LOUD's been proving that assumption wrong all stage.

## Where I Land

I still like NRG to win Friday's upper final, but I like 100 Thieves' floor more if they lose it, because a rematch against a scrappy LOUD side beats whatever G2 has left. Two NA teams control their own path to a bye. That almost never happens in this region, and it shouldn't happen quietly.
