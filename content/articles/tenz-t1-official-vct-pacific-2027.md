It's real. T1 announced on November 12 that TenZ is on the active roster for VCT Pacific 2027, and I'm glad I didn't have to wait any longer.

Last week I called this the best story of the offseason, if it happened. T1 confirmed crashies first, then posted TenZ with one line: he "begins a new chapter with T1." Stax, BuZz and iZu stay. That's the five.

## The slots are the tell

Pacific teams get two import spots. T1 used both on North Americans. That's the part I keep coming back to.

If this were only about selling jerseys, you'd sign TenZ and use the other spot on the best player money could buy. Instead they added crashies, a proven initiator now on his third region. You don't build like that for a highlight reel. You build like that to win.

Could I be wrong? Sure. A famous name on a Korean roster brings in eyes from every region, and T1 knows that better than anyone. I just think the crashies signing outweighs it.

## The role question

Nobody has said what TenZ plays. BuZz probably stays on duelist. Stax was last on smokes. iZu can anchor. That leaves TenZ on flex. He played Omen for Sentinels in 2024, so it isn't new to him.

When I came back to comp after a few months off, my aim was fine. My timings weren't. I was peeking a half second late every round and losing fights I used to win. That's the real test for TenZ. Two years away is a lot longer than a few months.

## What's next

Sheep Esports reports Sacy as head coach, but T1 hasn't confirmed it. The same report has his debut at Red Bull Home Ground on December 12 and 13. Also not confirmed.

I'll be watching either way.
