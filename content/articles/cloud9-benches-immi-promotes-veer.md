Three seasons, zero international appearances, and a six-game losing streak later, Cloud9 finally pulled the trigger on Immi. Except they didn't really pull it. He's still around, just one seat over now.

## The Move

Cloud9 announced it themselves on July 28: Ian "Immi" Harding moves to assistant coach, Wyatt "Veer" Blevins steps up to run the team. The org called it a unanimous decision from players and coaches. Maybe. It's also exactly the kind of framing that spreads the blame around instead of putting it all on the guy losing his title.

## Is This Actually About Immi?

Immi joined at the end of 2023. Nearly three years, zero trips to an international event, and a 5-11 record this season is a rough résumé no matter how you spin it. But there's a real argument going around right now, FNS made it well in a recent clip, that you're not beating a G2 or an NRG roster with the Cloud9 roster. Coaching buys you rounds. It doesn't fix a talent gap, and this region doesn't forgive a middling roster no matter who's drawing up the strats.

## The Part That Feels Rushed

Veer joined as an assistant in April. Four months later he's the head coach, four days before his debut against LOUD. That's not a development arc, that's an emergency plug. If it works, great story. If it doesn't, Cloud9 just burned a rebuild window on a coach with zero reps calling plays under playoff pressure.

## Where I Land

The record justified a change. I'm just not convinced swapping seats on the same staff, days before a must-win stretch, is the fix. One win over LOUD buys them time. Anything less, and this was a delay, not a decision.
