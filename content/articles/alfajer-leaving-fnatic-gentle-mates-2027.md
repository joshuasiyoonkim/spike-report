Alfajer leaving Fnatic is bigger than a normal roster move. Sheep Esports sources say he's set to join Gentle Mates for 2027. Nothing is official, so treat it as a report until the announcement lands.

## Why it hits

He joined Fnatic midway through 2022 and stayed more than four years. In this game, that's a lifetime. Most players don't survive two roster cycles on one team.

Fnatic already let Boaster walk, so this isn't a one-off. The core that defined that org is coming apart in one offseason.

## The weird part

Gentle Mates released their entire roster and coaching staff ahead of VCT 2027. They had nobody under contract. Sheep also reports Ruxic is leaving NaVi to join them.

So is this a team rebuilding or a team being built from scratch? I think it's the second one. Two names that big, signed onto an empty roster, means someone has a plan.

NaVi released their whole roster on October 28 after losing partner status, which is why Ruxic is even available. The partnership cuts keep shaking players loose, and the teams that move first get the pick of them.

## My read

Is leaving Fnatic for a rebuilt roster a risk? Yes. I've seen how a fresh five plays in the first few weeks of a split, and it's rarely clean. But after four years, staying put might have been the bigger risk.

I could be wrong if Gentle Mates can't fill out the rest of the lineup. A star duo with three question marks goes nowhere in EMEA.

Open qualifiers start this month. We'll find out fast.
