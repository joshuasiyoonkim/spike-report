Riot put real dates and real money behind the VCT 2027 overhaul this week. Open qualifiers start in November. That's the exact detail I said back in June wasn't public yet, and now it is.

## The Actual Structure

Americas, EMEA, and Pacific each send 12 teams to Kickoff: 8 partner slots, 4 through open qualifiers. But those qualifiers don't run by super-region. Brazil qualifies out of Brazil. Japan qualifies out of Japan. Turkey qualifies out of Turkey. A team wins its own country's bracket, then it's fighting partner orgs for a Kickoff seed with nothing handed to it. Most regions are also requiring three of five players to actually be from that region, so this isn't a loophole for stacked import rosters to farm a weak qualifier.

## The Number That Matters

This is the part I was actually worried about in June. A team that qualifies for Kickoff or a Cup earns $100,000. Make a Masters off that run, $200,000. Reach Champions, $400,000. That's not pocket change for a scrappy open-qualifier roster, that's the kind of payout that keeps a Tier 2 org from folding after one bad year. Free agency opens November 2, so rosters are already forming around this math before a single qualifier match gets played.

## Where I Land

In June I said this format only works if the open path isn't a pay-to-enter grind smaller orgs can't sustain. Real prize money attached to just qualifying, not just placing, is the actual answer to that question. I'm less cautious now than I was then. Somebody's five-stack out of a random national qualifier could be cashing a $400,000 check by next fall. That's the sport getting bigger, not just louder.
