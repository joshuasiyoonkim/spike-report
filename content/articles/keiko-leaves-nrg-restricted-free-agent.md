Keiko is out at NRG, and it's the wrong call.

He posted it himself on October 21. After Champions, NRG told him he won't continue with the team next year and is letting him explore options as a restricted free agent. His deal ran through 2027, so this was NRG's decision, not his.

## One season, one job

Keiko came over from Team Liquid to fill the hole s0m left. That's an impossible seat to sit in. He took it, helped the team to a runner-up at Esports World Cup 2026, and now he gets one year.

Is that a bad year? No. Is it the title-winning year NRG wanted? Also no. Both are true, and I still wouldn't cut the guy who just stabilized the most scrutinized role on the roster.

## The other reading

The fairer take is roles. Fans on VLR are pointing at interviews where bonkar talked about role issues, and a roster can be built around a different shape than the one Keiko fits. That could be it. I'm not inside the building.

But if it's roles, then NRG made a roster decision and sold it as a restricted free agency. Fine. Just say it.

## Where he lands

Keiko said he's open to offers from every region, and he can play duelist, smokes, sentinel, or flex. That's rare. Teams that missed out on the partnership cut and need a hard-to-replace utility player are going to call.

NRG will need a very good plan for that smokes slot. I haven't seen it yet.
