100 Thieves eliminated NRG 2-0, and I didn't see it going that cleanly. The defending champions are out in 5th-6th.

## Sunset was the series

Sunset went to overtime and 100T won it 14-12. NRG had real chances to steal it and didn't convert either overtime round.

I've lost enough overtime rounds in comp to know what happens next. You walk into the next map already doing math in your head. Haven finished 13-4, and that doesn't look like a team playing its own game.

## Is this a statement win?

Not really. This is the same 100T that needed three maps against Nongshim two days earlier, and G2 swept them in the upper quarterfinal. One good day doesn't erase that.

But it does change how I read them. They beat the reigning champs when a loss meant going home. Both maps. That's not nothing.

## What's next

100T play G2 on October 16 for a spot in the lower final. G2 already beat them 2-0 once this event, and the second map was a 13-11 squeaker.

Can 100T fix that in a week? I think they can. Having a rematch with that kind of time to prep is the best thing that could happen to them.
