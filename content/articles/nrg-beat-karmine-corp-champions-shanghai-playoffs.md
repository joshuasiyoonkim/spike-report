NRG are through to the Champions Shanghai playoffs. Karmine Corp took Ascent 13-7, NRG took Summit 13-11 and Haven 13-9, and the defending champs go to the top eight.

## The Ascent Loss Doesn't Worry Me

Was 13-7 a warning sign? On paper, sure. It was KC's pick and it looked like one. But NRG came into this series already holding a 2-0 over Nongshim, and a team that's comfortable enough to lose a map on the opponent's pick isn't panicking. They just went to their own pick and fixed it.

## Summit Was the Series

13-11 is the kind of map that decides a bracket. mada played Neon fast and decisive there, and that's the version of him I want to see in October. Haven then closed at 13-9 and it never felt close.

The funny part is that LewN was named MVP of the series for the losing side. That tells you how big his impact was on KC. It also tells you NRG won anyway, which is what champions do.

## Where I Land

Last week I wrote that no org has ever gone back-to-back at Champions. That was about history. This is about now. Losing a map and still sending a top team home is more convincing to me than a clean sweep would have been.

Playoffs start October 7. NRG have a week to rest and everyone else has a week to worry.
