Evil Geniuses are done, and the way it ended is worse than the fact that it ended.

## What Happened
EG announced on October 20 that they're closing their esports operations on November 2. CEO Chris DeAppolonio blamed "limited sponsorship and the rising cost of competing at the top level." The Valorant roster of dgzin, bao, zerona, Paincakes and supamen becomes free agents the same day.

The timing tells you the story. EG was left out of the VCT 2027 partnership list, along with Cloud9, ENVY, FlyQuest, FURIA and M80 in the Americas. Two days later they pulled the plug.

## The Part That Stings
EG beat Paper Rex 3-1 in the Champions 2023 final. That was the first international Valorant trophy for a North American org. Remember what that felt like? NA had spent years being the region that choked in the biggest series.

Then they never made another international event. So is the shutdown just what happens to an org that stopped winning? Partly. Their results dried up and the sponsors went with them.

But the other reading is harder to shrug off. The org that won Champions couldn't get a partner seat. A trophy bought them nothing in the room that counts.

## What's Next
I'm not sure this was avoidable. Maybe the org was already too thin to survive a missed cycle, and the partnership list just set the date.

The roster can't sign anywhere until November 2, so the free agent market opens that day. That's the part worth watching. Five players are about to hit the board at once.
