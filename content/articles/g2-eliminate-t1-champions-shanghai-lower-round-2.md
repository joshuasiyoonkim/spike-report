G2 beat T1 2-1 and I think they earned it more than the score says. T1 are out, and G2 are in the top four.

## The map that mattered

G2 took Lotus 13-11, T1 answered with Abyss 13-10, and then Ascent was never close. 13-6. G2's defense was clean, and T1 never found a way to make them panic.

That's the part I keep coming back to. When I drop a map I was winning, my next one gets sloppy. G2 lost Abyss and just reset. No tilt, no weird tempo.

## Jawgemo is carrying the entry work

Jawgemo finished the series with 58 kills and 14 first bloods. Is that sustainable? Probably not at that rate. But first bloods win decider maps, and G2 get theirs early.

## Next up

G2 face 100 Thieves on October 16 for a spot in the lower final. G2 already swept them 2-0 earlier this event, so 100T have had days to prep.

I still like G2 in that one. Not by a lot, though.
