Sentinels finished the rebuild, and keiko was the right last piece.

They announced him on November 16 at 1 PM PT and called him "the final piece." The starting five is keiko, johnqt, OXY, brawk, and canezerra, with Zellsis back as sixth man. Reduxx is out as an unrestricted free agent.

## NRG's loss, twice

When NRG let keiko go in October, I said teams would call. I didn't expect the call to come from the team that already took brawk.

Think about that. Two players from the NRG roster that made the Esports World Cup grand final now play for a direct Americas rival. NRG released one and lost the other. Sentinels just picked them up. The delay on keiko was his NRG contract, and once that cleared, Sentinels moved.

## Does the shape make sense?

On paper, yes. Reports have keiko on controller, brawk on initiator, and johnqt on sentinel. OXY takes space. That's a real role map, which is more than I could say about this team a year ago, when I spent most of their losses asking who was supposed to be doing what.

The worry is canezerra. He only got signed on November 6 after Riot shortened his ban, and he's the one starter without a top-tier résumé. If this roster has a soft spot, that's where teams will look first.

There's a fair reading that this is a superteam built in a month and it won't gel by kickoff. Maybe. But Sentinels' problem in 2026 wasn't talent mismatch. It was a roster with no clear structure. This one has structure before it has played a map.

## Where that leaves them

The Sentinels welcome video calls him Europe's best. That's marketing. What I care about is simpler. Sentinels now have a controller who can also flex, and they took him from the team they need to beat.

I'm in.
