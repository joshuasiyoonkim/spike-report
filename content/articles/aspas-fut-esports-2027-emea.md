Aspas picked the team over the money, and I respect it a lot.

On November 10, TheSpike reported that aspas reached a verbal agreement with FUT Esports for 2027. Sheep Esports backed it up later that day and said the deal between FUT and MIBR got done the same day. Nothing is signed or announced yet. But two outlets on the same story in one day is about as close as you get without a graphic.

When MIBR benched the whole roster in October, I said to watch where aspas lands first. Now we know. He's leaving Americas.

## Why FUT?

TheSpike says he turned down a seven-figure offer from a VCT China team. Sheep says he was open to China, but liked FUT's project more competitively.

That second part is the whole story. FUT topped the 2026 EMEA Championship Points table with 18 points and made its third straight Champions. That's a team that shows up every year. Aspas doesn't need another paycheck. He needs a roster that gets him back to the last weekend of an international event.

I've made the opposite call in a much smaller way. I've stuck with a duo who made the night easy instead of one who'd actually get me ranked up. It never felt good at the end of the act. Aspas went with the one that wins.

## The Part I'm Not Sure About

Sheep's expected lineup is aspas with Wo0t, hezacoil, batujnax and yetujey. Four Turkish players and Brazil's biggest star. Can that click by Kickoff? I don't know. A star joining an established core usually takes a split to settle, and EMEA Kickoff is expected in January.

There's also the contract. MIBR still has him through the end of 2027, so it isn't done until there's a signature.

Still, Americas loses its biggest name. EMEA gets him. I'll be watching FUT's first match.
