Two days ago I wrote that G2 was the team I'd bet my rent money on to disappoint me in Group C. Thursday in Shanghai, they didn't wait long to make me feel it. G2 opened Champions with a 2-0 sweep of TYLOO, 13-6 on Lotus and 13-3 on Sunset, and neither map was close after halftime.

## Trent Just Won the Group Stage Opener By Himself

Lotus was tied 6-6 at the half. Then Trent took over. He closed the series with 38 kills, 17 deaths, 20 assists, a 287 ACS, and a 91 percent KAST, and his Sunset alone was absurd: 22 kills against 6 deaths. That's not just a good map from an IGL. That's a player deciding the series isn't going five rounds either way.

## TYLOO Picked the Wrong Stage For a Debut

This was TYLOO's first trip to Champions, four of their five players stepping onto an international bracket for the first time, in front of a home crowd in Shanghai that wanted a story. Scales and Slowly both had moments, Scales especially picking fights in the mid-round chaos, but a home debut against a team playing this loose and this confident was never going to be gentle. TYLOO have three more group matches to learn how to play with that crowd instead of for it.

## Where I Land

I called G2 fourth in Santiago and fourth again here, and I'm not deleting that take yet. One map doesn't erase a group. But a team playing this carefree, this early, against a team that had the home-crowd edge on paper, tells me something. Group C just got a lot less quiet.
