100 Thieves are Stage 2 champions, and it took every map they had left in the tank to get there.

## Two Blowouts, Three Close Ones

Split went to LOUD 13-7, their attacking side doing exactly what it's done all playoffs. 100T answered by grinding out Sunset 13-11 and Ascent 14-12, the kind of scorelines that don't tell you how many rounds actually swung on a single read. Then LOUD reminded everyone why they were in this final at all, running Summit 13-1 to force a decider. Haven went to overtime tied at 12, and 100 Thieves closed it 14-12. Five maps, three of them within two rounds. That's not a trophy, that's a survival story.

## Asuna Didn't Blink

He played Phoenix on all five maps and walked away with an 85-81-24 stat line and 14 first kills, enough to take Finals MVP outright. Phoenix is not a subtle agent pick in a series this tight. Every flash, every wall, every ult had to land or the round was gone, and it kept landing. That kind of consistency across five maps in a decider is rare even at this level.

## Where I Land

I picked LOUD in four after they survived that reverse sweep scare against NRG. I said a team that claws back a 2-0 lead like that is terrifying in a final. Turns out terrifying wasn't enough. 100 Thieves are the ones lifting the trophy and heading to Shanghai as the top seed, their Champions berth already locked from Friday. I was wrong about the team. I wasn't wrong about the series going the distance.
