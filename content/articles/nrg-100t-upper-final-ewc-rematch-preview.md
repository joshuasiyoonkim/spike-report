I picked NRG in my bracket piece. I picked NRG again after the upper semis. Friday I'm taking 100 Thieves.

## Why I Keep Flip-Flopping

Both picks made sense in the moment. NRG group-stomped their way to a bye, then brawk went off for a 26/11/8 line and an Odin triple to bail out a shaky Ascent against LOUD. That's a team that finds a way. But Friday's opponent isn't LOUD, and I keep coming back to the last time these two actually met with something on the line.

## Paris Already Answered This

100 Thieves beat NRG 3-1 in the EWC grand final in July, and it wasn't a fluke scoreline. Cryocells took Finals MVP off a 212 ACS, right alongside Timotino's 218 and Asuna's 183. Three players over 180 ACS in a final is how you beat a roster that was supposed to be the best team alive, and NRG was that roster. That loss dropped NRG to a third straight international event without a trophy. I called it a pattern then. Nothing since has un-called it.

## The Season Series Doesn't Help Either

100T won the Kickoff meeting, NRG took Stage 1. Tied at one apiece before Paris. Add the EWC final and it's 2-1 100 Thieves in the matchups that mattered most, and the head-to-head under real pressure keeps going the same way.

## Where I Land

Winner goes straight to Sunday's grand final in São Paulo. Loser has to grind back through the lower final for another shot. That gap alone should be enough to make Friday feel massive, and it still might not be the biggest reason to watch.

100 Thieves in three. I'll take the ribbing if brawk finds another Odin.
