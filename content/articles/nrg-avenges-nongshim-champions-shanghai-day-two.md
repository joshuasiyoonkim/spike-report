Two months ago Nongshim RedForce knocked NRG out of Masters Santiago 2-0 in the upper final, then went on to win the whole thing. Friday in Shanghai, NRG got the rematch they were owed and didn't waste it. 13-5 on Lotus, 13-10 on Split, no third map needed.

## The Score Settling Wasn't Subtle

Two days ago I wrote that Group D was about whether anyone has an answer for brawk. Turns out the first answer of the tournament came from his own teammate. mada closed the series with 34 kills, a 1.39 rating, and a 9-3 record on first bloods, the kind of line that wins a series before the entry frags even start mattering. brawk still did brawk things, 32 kills against 23 deaths for a 227 ACS, but this wasn't his highlight reel. It was mada's.

## Santiago Actually Meant Something

It's easy to wave off a group stage rematch as just another best-of-three. This one had weight. Nongshim didn't just beat NRG in July, they made it look easy on the way to a title. NRG spent two months hearing about it. Lotus at 13-5 is the sound of a team that remembered.

## Where I Land

One group stage win doesn't erase Santiago and it definitely doesn't clinch Shanghai. Nongshim drops to the loser's match, one loss from elimination, and Group D still has KC and Xi Lai Gaming left to get through. But NRG walked in with a chip on their shoulder and played like it. That's the version of this team I'd worry about if I were anyone else in the group.
