Heretics didn't retool this offseason. They got gutted.

Wo0t's exit was confirmed this week, and he's not alone. Koshmaras and Boo are gone too. That leaves benjyfishy and RieNs as the only two players still on the roster, and both of their contracts expire November 2. Three spots to fill, and two of the two remaining guys aren't even locked in.

## Why Wo0t is the one that stings

Wo0t joined in October 2023 and stayed three years. In that time he picked up an EMEA title and an Esports World Cup trophy, plus six international appearances. That's the whole identity of the modern Heretics. You don't replace that with a good Tier 2 duelist and a press release.

## Is this a rebuild or a collapse?

Both readings are fair. The kind one says this was always going to happen once the partnership picture got settled, and Heretics are clearing the deck before free agency opens so they can shop smart. The harsh one says a team that won EMEA with Koshmaras earlier this year just threw away its chemistry for nothing.

I'm going with the harsh one. Chemistry is the hardest thing to buy, and they just let three pieces walk at once. If benjyfishy or RieNs leaves on November 2, this isn't a rebuild. It's a new team with an old logo.

The video is Koshmaras looking back at his run from Tier 3 to EMEA champion. Watch it with the context that he's no longer there.

Free agency opens in about a week. I'll believe in this roster when I see who they sign.
