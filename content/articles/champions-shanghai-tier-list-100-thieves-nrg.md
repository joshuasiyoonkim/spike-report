nAts sat down with f0rsakeN this week and built a full Champions Shanghai tier list. NRG went top four without much debate. 100 Thieves got top five, maybe top six, with nAts admitting he wasn't even sure why his gut said to slot them lower. I watched that part twice looking for the actual argument. There isn't one.

## The Scoreboard Says Otherwise

100 Thieves beat NRG 3-1 in the EWC grand final back in July, closing it out in overtime on the deciding map after NRG blew a 9-3 lead. Six weeks later, 100 Thieves swept NRG 2-0 in the Stage 2 Upper Final in São Paulo. Two matchups, both with something real on the line, both wins for 100T. I don't need a tier list to tell me who the better team is right now. I have a scoreboard, and it's not close.

## The One Fair Point in the Video

nAts did flag something worth taking seriously: 100T looked shaky in stretches of their most recent grand final against LOUD, and it's rare for any team to stay this sharp across three straight international finals. That's a real concern heading into a longer format in Shanghai. But "they might slip eventually" and "the team that's beaten you twice isn't top four" are two different claims. Only one of those holds up today.

## What This Means Going Into Shanghai

100 Thieves already drew the harder group, opening against T1 with JD Gaming waiting behind them and a home crowd on China's side. If that group goes how I expect, this tier list gets rewritten fast, and not in NRG's favor.

Book it: 100 Thieves are still the team to beat. Prove me wrong.
