Valyn going to Fnatic is a great move for him and a loss for Americas. Sheep Esports sources say he's set to join for 2027. Neither side has confirmed it, so treat it as a report until the announcement lands.

## How he got here

This isn't a player chasing a bigger brand. Valyn reportedly had a verbal agreement with LYON, conditional on the Mexican org landing a partnership slot. LYON wasn't one of the eight partner teams. The deal died with the slot, and he was suddenly free.

So the real story is that the format change shook loose an IGL that G2 had built around. He left right after Champions 2026.

## Why Fnatic

Is it a downgrade? Fnatic didn't qualify for Masters or Champions this year. On paper, that's a team going backwards.

I don't read it that way. The reported lineup is valyn, marteen, Minny, kaajak and Cloud, and a new IGL with a real roster to shape is a better pitch than a spot on a team in transition. Fnatic also confirmed they're staying in as a partner, so he gets stability that LYON couldn't offer.

I might be overrating the fit. A fresh IGL on a fresh roster can take a full split to click, and EMEA won't wait for him. But I'd rather bet on the player who's choosing the challenge.

The video is valyn after G2's loss to Fnatic at Masters Toronto. He called it a failure to adapt. Now he gets to be on the other side of that matchup.

Americas just lost its IGL to EMEA. Expect more of this once the open qualifiers start.
