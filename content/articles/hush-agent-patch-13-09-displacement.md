Hush looks fun for her user and miserable for everyone else. I'd rather be wrong about that than right.

## The Kit
She lands October 27 in Patch 13.09 with Season 26 Act VI. Flow puts down an anchor and launches her to it after a short delay. Flashbulb is a flash she steers toward her crosshair in the air. Her ultimate, Suspension, throws a grenade that levitates enemies and holds them in the air for a few seconds.

The ult is the loud one. Everyone will clip it. I don't think it's the problem.

## The Ability That Matters
Displacement is the signature. It's a steerable device that pulls nearby players toward its center, and anyone who collides with something gets concussed. It resets after two kills.

Is a pull ability actually strong? Alone, no. Paired with a steerable flash, yes. You drag a site defender off an angle, flash the spot he's headed for, and the duel is over before he knows which way is forward.

## My Read
I haven't played her yet, so this is the kit on paper and the trailer, nothing more. On paper she is a defender's worst night: the pull moves your crosshair, the flash blinds you where you land, and the reset rewards the team for converting.

The counter is probably boring. Stand still, hold a tight angle, and trade. That works until your team is the one getting dragged around.

The ult I'm fine with. A few seconds in the air is a long time, but you can see it coming. The pull you can't.

Ask me again after a week of comp.
