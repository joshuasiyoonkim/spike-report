The playoff draw gave us the one matchup I didn't want to pick: 100 Thieves vs G2, the only same-region series in the opening round.

## How G2 Got Here

G2 lost to Paper Rex 2-1 and had to win a decider against Liquid to survive. They did it 2-0, closing Abyss 13-7. BABYBAY went 41/26/12 across the series with a 1.34 rating, best on the server.

That's eight straight international playoffs for G2. I keep waiting for the run to feel less impressive. It doesn't.

## Why This Draw Hurts

100 Thieves went 2-0 in their group and earned the easier-looking bracket spot. Then the seeding handed them a team that already knows exactly how they play. Is that really an advantage? On paper, yes. In the server, I'm not sure. Familiarity cuts both ways, and G2 has nothing to lose after surviving a decider.

## My Pick

Two readings. Either 100T's group form carries over and they win clean, or G2 turns it into the grind they've won all event. I'm taking 100 Thieves, because a team that sweeps its group usually has the cleaner map pool. I could be wrong. G2 has made me wrong before.

Whoever loses drops into the lower bracket against a real team. Playoffs start October 7 at Jing'an Sports Center.
