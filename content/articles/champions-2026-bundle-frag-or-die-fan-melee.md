Riot dropped the trailer for "Frag or Die," the Champions 2026 bundle, and for once the gimmick isn't just the melee. It's the Phantom too.

## The Side-Swap Is the Real Feature

Every Champions bundle gets a signature melee, and this year's is a good one: a reverse-grip knife that folds out into a traditional Chinese folding fan. Fitting, since Shanghai's hosting Champions for the first time. But the part that actually made me sit up is the side-swap effect, which Riot's never done before. Play defense and the Phantom and melee sit in pearl white with cream-gold trim, a gold Champions aura, and a white dragon gun buddy. Switch to attack and the same weapons go onyx black with gold accents. Same gun, same round, different look depending on which half you're on. That's not a recolor variant you toggle in the menu. It's the game reading your side and swapping the skin live.

## The Price Question, Again

No official number yet, but early estimates put the full collection around 6,100 to 6,600 VP, in line with past Champions bundles and not far off what Aeris charged last month for its own transforming melee. The difference here is Riot's framing: a cut of Frag or Die sales goes back to VCT partner teams, so buying in during the two-week window before Champions wraps up actually puts money somewhere besides Riot's pocket. That's the closest thing to a discount justification I've seen on one of these.

## Where I Land

The transforming melee alone would've been enough to get written up. The side-swap tech is the part that's actually new, and it's a smarter use of the mechanic than another straight reskin. It drops September 22, right as groups kick off in Shanghai. I'm buying the Phantom. The knife-fan sells itself.
