100 Thieves are running it back. Asuna and Cryocells signed one-year extensions through 2027, and in an offseason where half of Americas is tearing it down, that's the smartest NA move so far.

## Is boring the right call?

Look around. Sentinels rebuilt. Cloud9 built a five out of tryouts. kiNgg left Leviatán after five years. Plenty of Americas teams start January from a blank page.

100T doesn't have to. This core won Stage 2 over LOUD in five maps, with Asuna on Phoenix all series and taking Finals MVP. It won the Esports World Cup 3-1 over NRG, with Cryocells taking Finals MVP on 212 ACS. First international trophy in org history. Then it finished third at Champions. You don't blow that up.

## The case against

There's a fair read the other way, and it's that Champions lower final. 100T took Split 13-5 off Vitality, then dropped the next three maps. A one-year deal could look like the org hedging instead of committing.

I don't buy it. Losing three straight maps to the eventual finalist is a prep problem, not a personnel problem. And one-year deals after a year like this just mean both sides wanted another one. Asuna goes into his seventh season with the team. Cryo goes into his fifth. That's not hedging. That's loyalty with a check-in date.

## Chemistry is the asset

I played most of last act in the same four-stack. We weren't the best aimers in any lobby. We won because I knew exactly when our Sova was about to dart and when our duelist was going to dry swing. You can't sign that. You build it.

Asuna and Cryo have years of it. bang, vora and Timotino haven't been officially confirmed for 2027 yet, so the full five isn't locked. The two pieces that matter most are.

The best NA team of 2026 kept its two best players. Easy call.
