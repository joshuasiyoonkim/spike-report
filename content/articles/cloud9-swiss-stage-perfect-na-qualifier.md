Cloud9's tryout roster is through to the NA qualifier playoffs without dropping a map. counterfeit 2-0 on November 20. Maryville University 2-0 on November 21. Two wins was all Swiss asked for, and they did it in four maps.

## Wasn't leadership the worry?

It was mine. When they signed alym, Derrek and nightz, I asked who takes the calls for a team built out of 10-player lobbies. Cloud9 answered before Swiss started: GLYPH stays as IGL. Fine on paper. The real test was the first close map.

They got one. Against Maryville, Cloud9 led their own map pick 8-4 at the half and then let it slip into overtime. That's the exact spot I said a tryout roster falls apart. They won it 14-12. Then they took Maryville's pick 13-5, and closed it with a flawless round set up by GLYPH using his ult to teleport into a corner behind them.

## Who's carrying it?

alym was MVP against Maryville, and the watch party up top shows why. He's the one taking first duels. That's what you sign an entry duelist from FURIA for. nightz on controller was my other concern, and in the highlights he's the name the casters keep coming back to.

## The other reading

counterfeit and Maryville came through the open path. Nobody in Swiss was a partner team, and blowing an 8-4 lead to a college team isn't nothing. I've had nights in comp where we won 13-5 and I still knew we got lucky on the first map.

I'm counting it anyway. A tryout team that wobbles and then closes overtime has learned something you can't learn in scrims.

The playoffs start December 1. Kickoff seats are on the line, and Cloud9 still hasn't lost a map.
