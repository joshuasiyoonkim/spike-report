I said Play-Ins was where I needed to actually see it, not just keep arguing for it. Saturday, I saw it, and it wasn't good. ENVY beat Sentinels 2-0 in the Upper Quarterfinal, and the bye everyone was excited about turned into one extra day to lose the same way.

## Two Maps, Same Story

Ascent went 13-10 ENVY. Summit wasn't close, 13-3. That's not a stolen pick or a server tilting the wrong way, that's a team getting outplayed on both sides of the map pool in a match they had extra prep time for. Four straight series losses now if you count the group stage skid, and this one came against a team plenty of people still rank below the top of Americas.

## Demon1 Made It Personal

The clip going around is Demon1 clutching up a multi-man situation on Summit against JonahP, reading the plant, timing the peek, and closing the round off almost no information. That's the kind of individual moment Sentinels used to be the team hitting, not eating. When your own city's namesake org can't find that gear anymore, that's the real story here, not just the scoreline.

## No More Room to Be Patient

They're in the lower bracket now, one elimination match from being out of Play-Ins entirely with real Champions points on the line. I said I'd hit my limit on patience if this run looked like group stage again. It does. cortezia and johnqt being good isn't the question anymore. Whether this roster can win a match that actually matters is.
