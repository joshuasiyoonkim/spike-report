I had this one wrong. Four days ago I wrote LOUD in four. Saturday into Sunday, 100 Thieves needed all five maps in São Paulo to win their first VCT Americas trophy, and I'm happy to eat the pick.

## The Match That Actually Lived Up to the Hype

Split went to LOUD 13-7, on 100 Thieves' own map pick. 100T answered on Sunset 13-11, then somehow clawed back into Ascent after falling behind 2-10, closing it out 14-12. LOUD reset with a 13-1 demolition on Summit that made the whole series feel like it was slipping away from 100 Thieves again. Then Haven, winner takes the trophy, went 14-12 their way. Asuna walked out with series MVP: 85 kills and 14 first bloods across five maps, an absurd number from someone who spent most of the year deferring to Cryo.

## Why This One Actually Mattered

100 Thieves have been the "should have won it" team for years. Asuna said as much in the post-match press conference, that he's not used to a season ending on a good note. This is the first regional trophy this core has actually lifted, and it happens to be the last VCT Americas title the format will ever hand out before next year's overhaul. There's something fitting about a team defined by heartbreak closing out an era by finally winning the thing, in front of a Brazilian crowd screaming for the other side.

## Where I Land

Back-to-back with their EWC win in July, 100 Thieves head into Champions Shanghai as the region's first seed, and I don't think that's a coincidence anymore. I was wrong about Sunday. I don't think I'll be wrong about them in Shanghai.
