Vitality swept G2 2-0, and the score hides how close G2 came to making this a series. The map they lost is the one I keep thinking about.

## Abyss was the whole series

G2 picked Abyss. Vitality led 9-2. I've been down 2-9 in comp and you stop playing to win, you play to not get embarrassed. G2 didn't do that. They clawed all the way back to 11-11.

Then Vitality closed it 13-11. That's what a team does when it has been to this stage before. G2 did everything right for nine rounds and still got nothing for it.

## Split was the other thing

Split was Vitality's pick and it finished 13-4. Once Abyss was gone, I don't think G2 had a second map in them. Losing a comeback like that takes something out of a team, and it showed.

## Is this a G2 problem or a Vitality problem?

Vitality problem for everyone else. G2 played fine. The first half of Abyss was the only real mistake in the series, and Vitality punished it harder than anyone has all event.

I could be overrating them. They needed three maps against Nongshim a round earlier. But this wasn't that team.

## What's next

G2 drop to the lower bracket, so they're not done, but they just lost the easy road. Vitality go to the upper final against LOUD on October 16, the team that just beat NRG 2-1.

That's the series I'm watching.
