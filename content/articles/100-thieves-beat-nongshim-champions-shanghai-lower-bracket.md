100 Thieves are still alive at Champions, and I'm happy about it. I'm just not convinced yet.

## What happened

They beat Nongshim RedForce 2-1 in a lower bracket elimination game. Summit went 13-5 to 100T. Nongshim took Sunset 13-9 on their own pick. Then Ascent ended 13-1.

Thirteen to one is not a normal scoreline in a series where the other team just won a map. Nongshim had not played Ascent in Shanghai, and it showed.

## Is that a good sign?

Two readings. One: 100T found their rhythm after the 2-0 loss to G2 the day before and finally played like the team that beat Nongshim at Esports World Cup. Two: Nongshim just had nothing left on a map they'd never touched.

I'm going with a mix, leaning toward the second. A decider that lopsided usually means one side checked out, not that the other side cooked. And 100T already had this exact series in their pocket once, at EWC, so it's not like they discovered something new.

## What I want to see

Nongshim are out in 7th-8th, and 100T move on. The next opponent will not hand them an Ascent they've never played. I want to see whether Summit, where they were comfortable, is the map they lean on or whether they can win on something unfamiliar.

Survive first. Get impressive later.
