Masterclass is a real team now, whether it wants to be or not.

s0m's stream team won all seven series 2-0 in NA Open Qualifier #1. That's 14 maps, zero dropped. They closed it on November 8 against Winthrop University, 13-10 on Sunset and 13-11 on Bind. The roster is s0m, Subroza, yay, Marved and florescent, with FNS coaching. Their qualifier playoffs run starts November 20.

## Is 14-0 Real?

Against open bracket teams, it's what a lineup with this much experience should do. s0m won Champions 2025 with NRG. Subroza, yay and Marved have all played at the top level. The question was never whether they could beat Tier 2 teams that haven't figured themselves out yet.

The question is ENVY, Cloud9 and Shopify Rebellion Black, who are all waiting in the 16-team field. Zikz points it out in the breakdown above: ENVY has been scrimming for a long time. Masterclass reportedly got three days of practice before the qualifier. I'd bet they've played more official maps on stream than in scrims.

## The No-Prac Problem

I've lived this. My best comp nights come with the same five-stack, everyone talking, nobody needing a plan. Then we hit a team that actually had one, and we lost a round on a retake we'd never drilled. Pug calling gets you far. It doesn't get you a set play at 11-11.

That's what Zikz flagged, too. s0m calls everything live, and their structured rounds looked rough. He also pulled up yay's first duels on Chamber. He's often behind s0m and florescent, which is a strange way to use yay.

## Where I Land

The other reading is that the stream team is loose because it doesn't practice, and practicing turns it into a pressure cooker. Zikz says something close to that. I don't buy it. A few four-hour scrim blocks won't kill the vibe. They'll give s0m a set play when the game gets close.

Zikz has them top four. I think that's right. But only if they practice.
