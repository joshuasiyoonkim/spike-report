Friday in São Paulo went exactly like July in Paris. NRG and 100 Thieves played for something real, and 100 Thieves walked away with it again.

## The Score Says It Wasn't Close

100T took Abyss 13-3 and closed out Sunset 13-6 to sweep the Upper Final 2-0, which means they skip the lower bracket grind entirely and walk straight into Sunday's grand final. NRG drops to Saturday's lower final against whoever survives G2 and LOUD, needing to win two more series in three days just to get back to where 100 Thieves already is. That's the brutal math of double elimination when you lose the match that actually matters.

## Same Two Teams, Same Ending

This is the second time in two months these two have played a winner-take-something match, and the second time 100 Thieves has won it. Cryocells was Finals MVP when 100T beat NRG 3-1 for the EWC trophy back in July, and he was doing damage again on Friday, this time closing out a bracket door instead of a trophy lift. I wrote yesterday that NRG's last loss to this exact matchup would sharpen them, not break them. I was wrong. Sharpened teams don't get run out 13-3 on their own map pick.

## Where I Land

100 Thieves are headed to Shanghai, their first Champions appearance since 2022, and they're getting there by beating the same team twice with a trophy on the line. I picked NRG both times. I'm not doing it a third time. If these two meet again this year, I'm taking 100T and living with whatever that says about me.
