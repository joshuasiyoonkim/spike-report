Zekken is done at MIBR, and if the NRG reports hold, it's the best landing spot he could have found.

MIBR made it official on November 19 with a short farewell video and a thank-you message in Portuguese. He joined in December 2025 on a deal that ran through 2027. He got one season. The whole roster had already been moved to inactive back on October 23, so this was the paperwork catching up.

## What went wrong in Brazil?

The superteam never showed up when it counted. MIBR finished 5th-6th in Stage 2 and missed Champions Shanghai entirely. On paper, nobody in the region was supposed to handle aspas and zekken on the same server. NRG did. They beat MIBR 2-1 in Kickoff and then reverse swept them to book Masters Santiago.

The video up top is tarik reacting to NRG against MIBR this year, asking if NRG could stop aspas and zekken. Funny how it reads now.

## Why NRG fits

Sheep Esports reports zekken has a verbal agreement with NRG for 2027. That's a report, not an announcement, and NRG hasn't posted anything yet.

But look at the roster. As of now it's mada, skuba, Ethan and eeiu. Keiko's old slot is the one empty chair, and keiko is in Sentinels colors now. NRG needs a fifth who can carry rounds without a system built around him. Zekken won Masters Madrid with Sentinels doing exactly that.

## The other reading

You could argue NRG is buying the name off a failed season. Two firepower players walked out the door this offseason, and swapping in a guy coming off a bad year is a gamble.

I'd take that gamble. MIBR's problems were bigger than one player, and an all-NA roster takes away the communication question entirely.
