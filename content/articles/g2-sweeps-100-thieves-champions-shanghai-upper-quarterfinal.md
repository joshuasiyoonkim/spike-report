G2 beat 100 Thieves 2-0 and it wasn't close in the way that counts. The series was won on team play, and I didn't see that coming.

## Abyss was a clinic

13-5. G2 used fakes all map, and 100T kept rotating off sites that were never getting hit. If you've ever been the guy sprinting from A to B for the fourth time in a half, you know that feeling. You stop trusting your own read.

Leaf finished with 19 kills and 11 deaths, but the number that stuck with me is team-wide. Four of G2's five cleared 200 ACS. That's not one player carrying. That's five.

## Ascent was the real test

13-11, tied 6-6 at the half. This is where 100T usually gets you. They won Americas Stage 2 and they're the champs of the region. G2 now leads the all-time head-to-head 6-4.

Babybay hit a 1v2 clutch that helped close it out. G2 won five clutches across the series. A team that wins that many in two maps is either lucky or calm, and I don't think it was luck.

## Is this the breakthrough?

I'm not sure yet. G2 lost to Paper Rex 2-1 earlier in the event, and one clean series doesn't erase that. But beating the reigning Americas champions without needing a hero game is a different kind of win.

100T drop to the lower bracket. They're not out. G2 move on to the upper semifinals.
