Leviatán just won the offseason in Americas. Neon, blowz, spikeziN, lukxo and erde. That's the 2027 roster, completed on November 20, and two of those names lifted the Champions trophy in Shanghai a month ago.

## What did LEV actually lose?

A week ago I said LEV had a blank page. kiNgg was gone after five years, Sato was gone with him, and the Masters London core was down to three. That's a team that usually spends a year finding itself.

Instead they kept the three who stayed, gave Neon, blowz and spikeziN two-year extensions, and went shopping at the one place nobody expected: the team that just won the world.

## Why these two?

erde is 18 and the youngest Champions MVP ever. 310 kills across 18 maps in Shanghai. lukxo is 19. These aren't veterans cashing out. They're two of the best young players in the region picking a project over a jersey.

lukxo's video up top is worth the watch. He said it was a decision made by "lukxo the son," not just lukxo the player, and that he didn't know who would stay at LOUD for 2027. That second part matters. He wasn't leaving a dynasty. He was leaving a roster that was already coming apart.

## The other reading

You could say LEV just bought firepower and forgot the brain. kiNgg called everything for that team, and nobody on this list has his reps as an IGL. That's the real question, and I don't have the answer yet.

I still like it. I've played with a shotcaller who knew everything and teammates who couldn't hit a shot, and I've played the reverse. The reverse wins more games in my rank. I think it wins in pro too.

We find out fast. They debut at the Radiant International Invitational in China on December 6, against Vitality and Gen.G.
