Global Esports is out of Valorant. Five weeks after winning VCT Pacific Stage 2, the whole roster is free agents and the org is pivoting to an AI media venture called GE Studios.

## What Happened

Founder Rushindra Sinha posted the farewell on October 19. Kr1stal, UdoTan, autumn, xavi8k, and PatMen are all on the market. Sinha said GE had told Riot that 2026 would most likely be its last year in esports, and that the new partnership terms made it hard to keep the roster together.

## Two Readings

Is this a business that never worked, or a business the new system priced out? Probably both. But I lean toward the second, and here's why. This year they became the first Indian org at Champions. In September they reverse-swept Nongshim from 0-2 to win Pacific. You don't get a trophy run like that from a team that's been circling the drain. You get it from an org that found its feet right as the ground moved.

I could be wrong. Sinha said they saw this coming, which means the title run might have been a goodbye lap, not a breakthrough. That's a sadder story, and it fits his own words better.

## Where I Land

The part that sticks with me is PatMen. He won Pacific Stage 2 with Paper Rex last year and again with GE this year, and now he's looking for a team in November, right when free agency opens. Somebody's going to sign the guy who wins finals.

The best result the org ever had, and it ended weeks later. That's the story.
