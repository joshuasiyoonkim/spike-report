Sentinels' clean start to Stage 2 group play ended Sunday. G2 took it 2-1, Sentinels grabbing Breeze, G2 answering on Lotus, and Split going the distance to decide it. First loss of the stage for a team that looked untouchable two weeks ago.

The story of that series is babybay. 216 ACS, 38/30/7, 140 ADR across the three maps, and every big number came with a bigger moment attached: the read on Sentinels' A-main split in game one, the operator picks that kept turning their setups into dead reads. None of it mattered more than the last round of Split. G2 on match point, both teams down to their rifles and one operator each, and it turned into babybay against johnqt with the guns that matter most. babybay won it. Series over.

## The Vet Answers the Question Himself

I saw the headline going around a few weeks back, babybay talking about competing in his 30s, saying he can still play against anybody. Read like the kind of thing a veteran says to keep the doubt quiet. Sunday wasn't him saying it. It was him proving it, in the one round where the whole match sat on his aim.

## Sentinels Aren't Cracked, They're Tested

One loss doesn't undo two weeks of good results. Sentinels are still sitting near the top of Group Omega with plenty of stage left, and dropping a three-map fight to a G2 roster that's been quietly good all year isn't a crisis. But it's the first real answer to whether this roster can lose a close one and stay level. We find out next series.
