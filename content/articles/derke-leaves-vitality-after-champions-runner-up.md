Derke leaving Vitality is the right call for both sides, even if it looks backwards.

## Why does it feel wrong?

Vitality just played in their first Champions grand final. LOUD beat them 3-1 on Sunday, October 18, and Vitality walked away with $400,000 and a runner-up medal. Derke was a big reason they got there. Six days later, the team announced he's gone ahead of 2027.

Two seasons, one VCT trophy, and a grand final. Vitality's own farewell post credited him with the org's first of both. That's a real legacy.

## So why leave?

I see two readings. One: the roster peaked, and you sell high. Two: Derke wants a fresh start after coming up one series short of the thing he's chased his whole career.

I'm picking the second. Remember how he looked at Masters London, 44 kills in a 2-0 sweep of Heretics? That's a player at his ceiling. When you're at your ceiling and the trophy still slipped away, staying put and running it back feels like a trap. I might be wrong and it could just be money, but the timing points at motivation.

## What now?

No destination is announced. The transfer window opens November 2, and Derke is going to be the most talked-about name on the market. Every EMEA team that missed the grand final will be calling.

Vitality, meanwhile, lose a duelist who put up a 231 ACS in the lower final against 100 Thieves. You don't replace that with a shrug.
