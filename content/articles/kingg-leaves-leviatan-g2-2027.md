kiNgg is done at Leviatán. He posted his farewell today, and it closes out a run that started in November 2021. Five years, one org, one trophy that should have been worth more than it was.

## Why now?

Is it a surprise? Not really. Sheep Esports reported weeks ago that he was set to join G2 for 2027, alongside his LEV teammate Sato. Neither G2 nor kiNgg has confirmed where he's going, so the destination is still a report. The exit isn't.

The timing makes sense once you remember September. Leviatán won Masters London in June, beat Paper Rex 3-2 in the final, and then missed Champions anyway. 19 points to G2's 21. They lost to G2 in the lower bracket in a series they led after Split. If I'd built a roster for five years and watched the standings math erase a Masters title, I'd be looking around too.

## The G2 read

There are two ways to see this. One: G2 is poaching the guy who knocked them around all year. Two: G2 is buying the exact thing it just lost. Valyn reportedly went to Fnatic, jawgemo is officially at Vitality, and the reported 2027 lineup has trent and leaf staying with HYUNMIN coming over from Pacific.

I'm going with the second. G2 lost its IGL. kiNgg is the best one available in Americas, and he's bringing his own entry. That's not poaching. That's replacing a brain with a better-proven brain.

## The catch

I've switched duo partners mid-season before. Same rank, same agents, and we still lost three straight because we didn't know each other's timings. kiNgg's whole edge at LEV was five years of shared timings. He doesn't bring those to G2. Only Sato does.

I still think G2 just got better. LEV just got a blank page.
