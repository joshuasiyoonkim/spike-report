MIBR benched the whole team, and I think it's a reset, not a disbanding.

On October 23 the org moved aspas, zekken, Verno, Mazino and tex to inactive ahead of 2027. The stated reason was the regional transition and the challenges that come with it. The staff stays: fRoD, Happy and the manager are all still on the books.

A few videos are calling this a total disband. That's not what the statement says. "All existing contractual obligations remain in effect," and any transfer gets negotiated with MIBR directly. Tex's deal ends this year. The other four are signed through the end of 2027.

## Why do it?

There are two readings. One is that MIBR is cutting the cord. The other is that it's clearing the deck so it can rebuild under the new rules.

I'm taking the second. MIBR is reportedly one of the eight Americas partners for 2027-28, so the slot isn't going anywhere. A roster with one Brazilian on it doesn't fit a regional setup that caps imports. That's a roster problem, not an org problem.

I could be wrong about that. If MIBR just wanted out, it wouldn't be keeping a coaching staff.

## The part that bugs me

Aspas and zekken were the whole identity of this team. Nobody gets a farewell here. They just show up on a transfer list.

Now the contracts are the story. Any team that wants either one pays MIBR, and MIBR is in no hurry.

Watch where aspas lands first. That tells us how this goes.
