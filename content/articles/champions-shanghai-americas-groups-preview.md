Champions starts Thursday, and I keep coming back to the same feeling: two of my three Americas teams got the group they were trying to avoid.

## 100 Thieves, Group A

Winning Stage 2 in São Paulo was supposed to earn 100 Thieves a break. Instead they open against T1, then have to get through JD Gaming and FUT Esports too. T1 is the Pacific team that always plays above what their league record says at LAN. JD Gaming is China's top seed, in Shanghai, in front of a crowd that's going to be deafening on every clutch. I like 100 Thieves. I don't love this draw.

## NRG, Group D

The defending champions landed with Nongshim RedForce, who just won Masters Santiago and haven't looked human since. Karmine Corp and Xi Lai Gaming round out the group, and neither is a gimme, but Nongshim is the fight that decides whether NRG defends the trophy or exits groups with it. I picked NRG all through Stage 2 and I'm not backing off now. They lost the big one to 100 Thieves in July and came back sharper. I need to see that again here.

## G2, Group C

Nobody's talking about G2's group and that's the trap. TYLOO, Team Liquid, and Paper Rex isn't easy, it's just quiet. PRX has been one of the most consistent teams on the year. G2 took fourth in Santiago with this same core. Fourth isn't good enough anymore.

## Where I Land

100 Thieves survives on talent alone. NRG needed a bad loss to get here, so I trust the fix. G2 is the team I'd bet my rent money on to disappoint me. Thursday tells me how wrong I am.
