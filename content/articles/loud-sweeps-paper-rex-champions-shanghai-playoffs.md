LOUD didn't just beat Paper Rex. They beat them twice from behind, and that's the part that should worry NRG.

## Two maps, two holes

Split went 13-9 to LOUD after PRX jumped out to an 8-4 lead. Fine. Maps swing.

Sunset is the one. Paper Rex was up 9-3 at the half on LOUD's own pick. I've been in that spot in comp, on the wrong side, and you start planning your next queue. LOUD went the other way. They stopped trading blind, started playing proactive on defense, and dragged it to overtime. Final was 15-13.

Paper Rex got one real chance to close it. Something hit a 1v2 clutch in the first overtime. Then the round after, it fell apart again.

## Was that a PRX choke or a LOUD adjustment?

Both, but I'm leaning LOUD. A 9-3 lead doesn't disappear on errors alone. Somebody has to punish them, and LOUD did.

I could be wrong. PRX looked unbeatable in groups, beating both Liquid and G2 2-1. If this was one bad half, I'll eat that. Two comebacks in one series doesn't read like luck though.

## What's next

PRX drops to the lower bracket, so they're not out. LOUD moves to the upper semifinal, which is NRG, the defending champs, fresh off their own 2-0 over T1.

NRG gets a team that doesn't think it's losing at 9-3. Good luck.
