NRG made their first real answer to losing half their roster, and it's a good one.

They announced eeiu on November 18 with a simple post: "They didn't bring back Dan, so we did. Welcome back @777eeiu." It's a return. NRG first signed him in October 2020, and four years after he left, he's back to fill the initiator spot brawk took to Sentinels.

## Why it's a risk

FURIA pulled him from the active five on July 19. He played one Stage 2 match, a 1-2 loss to LEVIATÁN, and then C0M had his spot. FURIA never gave a reason. Lion Esports reported disagreements over communication, leadership, and mid-round calls, and those are anonymous-source claims.

So is he a problem in the server? On paper, maybe. In the results, no. He won VCT Americas Kickoff 2026 with FURIA and took them to Masters Santiago. Before that, he won Stage 1 2024 with 100 Thieves and finished fourth at Masters Shanghai. That's two different orgs where he was part of the best team in the region.

## The other reading

There's a fair version where NRG just signed the guy nobody else would close on. Cloud9 was reportedly interested and didn't land him. Maybe that means something.

I don't buy it. The same Lion report said his individual level was among the highest on FURIA. Disagreements about how a team calls mid-round are fixable with a different coach and a different voice. A lack of aim isn't.

## What's left

As of now it's mada, skuba, Ethan, and eeiu, with keiko's old slot still open. That fifth signing decides whether this is a contender or a rebuild with good initiators.
