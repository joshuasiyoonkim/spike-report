Riot's own dev update calls this one "big," and for once that's not just marketing copy. Patch 13.06 goes live September 22 with a brand new rifle, a brand new mode, and a full Agent Mastery overhaul. Two days later, Champions Shanghai groups start. That timing is the actual story here, more than any single change in the notes.

## The Warden Changes the Rifle Math

For years the 2900-credit slot has been Phantom or Vandal, full stop. Now there's a third option. Warden trades a slower 6.5 rounds per second and an 18-round mag for a real 2x ADS scope and 50 body damage with zero falloff at range. That's a rifle built for someone who wants sniper-adjacent range control without giving up full auto. I'm curious how fast comps start building around it, because that's a genuinely new decision at the buy menu for the first time in years.

## Gauntlet: Glitched Is the Wildcard

Eight teams of two, Robo-Agents built from randomized ability pools, matches over in under fifteen minutes. This isn't trying to be competitive VALORANT. It's Riot testing how weird the sandbox can get without breaking the core game, and I respect them throwing it out there instead of soft launching it quietly into a random Tuesday.

## Where I Land

A new weapon landing two days before the biggest event of the year is either supreme confidence or an odd sense of timing. Pros get almost no real reps with the Warden before Shanghai cameras turn on, which means groups could open with everyone still figuring out a gun that didn't exist a week earlier. I like the swing. I just wouldn't bet on seeing much Warden on stage at Champions itself.
