Patch 13.10 is a good Sentinel patch. It doesn't look like one on paper, but it is.

## What actually changed?

Cypher's Spycam, Killjoy's Turret and Vyse's Arc Rose now come back 45 seconds after being destroyed instead of 60. That's it for the headline. Cypher also pays for it: Spycam's reclaim cooldown goes up from 15 to 20 seconds.

So is 15 seconds a real buff? On paper, barely. In the server, it's the difference between having your util for the retake or not.

## Why 15 seconds matters

Think about when your turret actually dies. It's usually in the first 30 seconds of a round, when somebody clears it before they hit the site. With a 60 second cooldown, it came back right around the time the spike was already down and the round was decided. At 45, it's back for the post-plant or the retake. That's the window Sentinels exist for.

I'm a Killjoy player when my team lets me, and the old cooldown always made me feel like I had one job per round. Lose the turret early and I was a rifle with an alarm bot. Now there's a second act.

## The Cypher catch

The reclaim nerf is the part people will skip, and they shouldn't. Cypher players who pick up the cam and move it every round are paying 5 extra seconds for that habit. If you play a fixed setup, you win. If you reposition a lot, it's closer to a wash. I think Riot wants Cypher committing to a spot, and this is how they say it.

The video above runs through the rest, including the tighter assist rules. The cooldown is the change that'll show up in your games.
