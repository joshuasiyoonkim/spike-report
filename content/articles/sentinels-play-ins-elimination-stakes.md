Stage 2 group play is done for Sentinels, and the record says what it says: they opened 2-0, then dropped three straight to close it out, the last one to a KRÜ team that hadn't won a series all stage. Play-Ins starts tomorrow. This is where it gets real.

## Why This Round Actually Matters

Sentinels are sitting low on championship points, in the same crowded bottom tier as ENVY, Cloud9, and EG. That matters because there are two ways into Champions Shanghai this year: win Stage 2 Playoffs outright, or climb the points table across the season. After three losses in a row, neither path looks close right now. Play-Ins is the tournament where that has to start changing, or the math gets ugly fast.

## The Bye Is a Gift, Not a Guarantee

Sentinels open in the Upper Quarterfinal on Saturday, skipping the Round 1 gauntlet some of these teams have to survive first. That's real value, an extra day to fix whatever's been going wrong. But I don't think this team can afford to look like the group stage version of itself a fourth series in a row. A bye buys prep time. It doesn't buy patience.

## Where I Land

I've written twice now that I believe in this roster on talent, and I'm not walking that back completely. johnqt being healthy still matters. cortezia has quietly been the best player wearing the jersey this stretch. But belief only survives so many losses before it turns into denial, and I've hit that point. Play-Ins is the round where I need to actually see it, not just keep arguing for it.
