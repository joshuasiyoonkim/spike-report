Sentinels are good again on paper. I know how that sounds after this year, so let me explain why I'm buying it.

## Two Signings, Two Days

OXY came first. Sentinels announced the former Cloud9 duelist on November 2, after Sheep Esports had it more than a week earlier. Then on November 3 they added brawk, the Champions 2025 MVP, who walked away from NRG. Two names, one day apart, both replacing the Jerrwin era.

## Why It's Different This Time

Last time Sentinels rebuilt around a star who was supposed to arrive and never really did. Remember the TenZ wait? That cost them a whole season of planning. This time the pieces are actually on the roster before the year starts. An initiator with a world title MVP and a duelist who can take space on his own. JohnQT stays as the anchor, and Redux and cortezia are reportedly in the mix too.

Is it a perfect lineup? No. Some outlets say the five is complete and others say a spot is still open, and I'd wait for the official roster before calling it done. Sentinels also missed every international event in 2026, and brawk can't fix a team that stopped communicating by itself.

## What I'm Watching

Honestly, the NRG side hurts me more than the Sentinels side helps me. NRG lose the Champions 2025 MVP to a rival in their own region. That's a bigger story in Americas than people are treating it.

Sentinels fans got burned last year. Mine is cautious optimism, and I'll take it.
