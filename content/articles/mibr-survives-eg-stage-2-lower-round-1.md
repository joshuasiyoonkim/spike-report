I picked MIBR to fall to LEVIATÁN in Upper Round 1 and I was basically right, a clean sweep instead of the three games I called. What I didn't see coming was Saturday's real scare, a Lower Round 1 match against an Evil Geniuses roster stacked with rookies that had no business making this close.

## Lotus Was the Warning

EG opened on MIBR's own map pick, Lotus, and took it with a comp nobody had scouted off them all stage. That's the same trick that's kept this team alive since play-ins: gamble the pick, bank on the tape not existing yet. It worked, and MIBR looked rattled walking into map two.

## The Thrifty That Saved the Season

Sunset should have been a sweep. EG won the pistol, built the lead, had every reason to close it out. Instead MIBR ripped off a thrifty round that flipped the entire map's momentum, and by the time the round counter caught up they'd stolen it back. EG's rookie duelist Da Jazine, playing this week in front of his own hometown crowd, put together the kind of series that gets remembered even in a loss. He just ran out of teammates by Haven.

## Verno Closes It, Barely

Haven went the distance. MIBR needed a late Odin from Verno to shut the door, and IGL Mazino said as much after: "after first map it was really stressful and then the two after they were like really tough too." That's a captain being honest instead of spinning a 2-1 into a statement win.

## Where I Land

MIBR survives, but Saturday's tape doesn't look like a team built for Shanghai. It looks like a team that got out. LOUD is next, today, and I'm not confident this version of MIBR beats them.
