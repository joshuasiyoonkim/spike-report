Immi's gone, and Cloud9 lost anyway.

Riot's most miserable roster of 2026 benched head coach Immi on July 28 and handed the reins to his assistant, Veer, after a 5-11 season and six straight losses. Three days later Veer got his first game, Haven against LOUD, and Cloud9 actually opened it right. 13-8. First time in a while I watched a Cloud9 map and thought, okay, maybe this is the reset. Then Sunset happened. Then Summit happened. 8-13 both times. Series over, 1-2, streak now at seven.

## Is the Coach Actually the Problem?

I don't think it was ever really about Immi. Tactical Rab said it well in his rundown of the move: this roster's been through Vic leaving and coming back, Oxi stepping away and returning, Zepper gone, Fireball and Jack in. That's not a coaching staff issue, that's a team that hasn't had a stable identity in months. Benching Immi is the easy move because it's the one Riot notices on a partnership review. It says "we're trying" without touching the harder question of whether this roster fits together at all.

## The Haven Map Is the Cruelest Part

If Cloud9 just got run over 13-8, 13-8, 13-8, this is a non-story, bad team loses badly, day ends. Instead they won map one clean, then dropped Sunset and Summit 8-13 each, just 16 rounds won across 42 played the rest of the way. That's not a talent gap. That's a team that still doesn't know how to close.

Veer's honeymoon lasted one map. The real test isn't this weekend, it's whether Cloud9 looks any different by week five.
