Five Champions, five different trophies. Acend in 2021, LOUD in 2022, Evil Geniuses in 2023, EDward Gaming in 2024, NRG last year in Paris. Nobody has ever walked in as the defending champion and walked out with the same result. NRG gets the first real shot at it, starting tomorrow.

## What They're Defending

Paris wasn't close in the ways that matter. NRG went undefeated from groups through the final, then beat Fnatic 3-2 on their own stage to close it out. brawk took MVP, and Ethan became the first player ever to win two Champions, one with Evil Geniuses in 2023 and one with NRG last year. That's a resume. It just doesn't come with a bonus life at Shanghai. The bracket doesn't care what happened in July.

## The Target Is on brawk

A video dropped today calling him this year's Champions villain, and watching the clips, I get it. Other teams are changing their whole map plan around whatever he's holding, usually an Odin nobody else on earth is this good with. When the entire scouting report on a player is "there's no answer, just hope he misses," that's not a compliment. That's dread. Every team in Group D knows exactly who they have to get through.

## Where I Land

Group D is Nongshim RedForce, KC, and Xi Lai Gaming, none of them a gimme. But I don't think the story of Shanghai is whether NRG survives groups. I think it's whether anyone in this field has an answer for brawk when it matters. Nobody has all year. History says somebody has to eventually. I'm not betting it's this month.
