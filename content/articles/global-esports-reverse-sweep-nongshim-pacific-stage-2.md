Three months ago I called Global Esports firing FrosT four days before EWC a strange call. Historic first international run, coach gone, an analyst promoted into the deep end. This week Global Esports won the VCT Pacific Stage 2 title, reverse-sweeping Nongshim RedForce from 0-2 down. Strange call doesn't look so strange anymore.

## The Comeback

Nongshim came in fresh off winning Masters Santiago and took the first two maps of the grand final. Then GE won three straight to close it out: Abyss 13-10, Lotus 13-5, and Haven 13-10 in the decider. That's not a team hanging on for survival. That's a team that found something mid-series and didn't let go of it. Doing that against the reigning Masters champions, in a final, is the kind of stretch that decides how a whole year gets remembered.

## Patmen, Two Orgs, Two Trophies

Patmen just won back-to-back Stage 2 playoffs on two different rosters, Paper Rex last year, Global Esports this year. That's not one great team carrying a player through a bracket. That's a player who shows up specifically when the games get short and the stakes get real, regardless of who's next to him.

## Where I Land

I wrote in June that firing the coach who got GE their first-ever international appearance, four days before that same tournament, made no sense on paper. I still think the timing was reckless. But Daniel "Platoon" Zhou, the analyst who got thrown into that seat, just walked an org that couldn't clear the league stage for years to a Pacific title over the region's best team. I don't know if that makes the move right. I know I can't call it a disaster anymore.
