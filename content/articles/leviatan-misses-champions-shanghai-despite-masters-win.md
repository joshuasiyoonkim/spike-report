Leviatán is not walking onto a stage in Shanghai this month. That's the actual headline sitting under all the group draw and tier list noise. A team that beat Paper Rex 3-2 in the Masters London grand final in June, the hardest trophy in this sport outside of Champions itself, does not have a Champions Shanghai seed. First team in VCT history to pull that off.

## How You Actually Miss This

Not by collapsing. Leviatán ran into 100 Thieves in the Stage 2 upper semifinal on August 28 and lost 2-0, then ran into G2 in the lower bracket two days later and lost 2-1, a series they actually led after taking Split. G2 finished the year with 21 championship points. Leviatán finished with 19. Two points, roughly the width of one dropped map in São Paulo, is the entire gap between playing for a world title and watching it from a couch.

## The Points System Doesn't Know What a Trophy Is

I get rewarding a full season instead of one good week. I don't get a system where winning the second hardest event in the sport is worth less than stacking quarterfinal finishes at events nobody remembers by August. kiNgg built this roster into one that beat Heretics, XLG, Vitality, and EDG on the way to London, then beat Paper Rex in a fifth map decider. None of that mattered two months later.

## Where I Land

Trophies are supposed to outweigh standings math, not lose to it. Leviatán is the team finding that out in real time, and they're not walking away with anything to show for June except the trophy itself. Fix the points before this happens to someone else.
