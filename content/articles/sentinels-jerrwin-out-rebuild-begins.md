Jerrwin posted the standard offseason line yesterday: still under contract, but Sentinels are letting him explore options for 2027. Nobody in his replies treated it as a maybe. JohnQT wished him luck. Redux called it "super fun playing with you, bro." That's not the language teammates use when a spot is actually still open.

## One Season Wasn't the Plan

Sentinels reportedly had him signed through 2028, and by most accounts they were hoping TenZ would come back first. When that never happened, they settled for Jerrwin instead. Visa issues delayed his debut, and the team never really found its footing after that: 2-3 out of the Stage 1 groups, a three-game skid to close Stage 2, then a Play-Ins exit to ENVY where JonahP admitted the team looked "scared and too confident at the same time" and couldn't explain why. I said after that loss I'd hit my limit on excuses for this roster. This is the offseason where the roster agreed with me.

## What's Actually Confirmed

2026 is the first year since 2023 that Sentinels have missed every international event. JohnQT stays, that part isn't in question after the year he had. Everything else is open. Redux has never played a best-of-five and was supposed to be the future at some point, but he didn't play like it down the stretch. Cortezia's future isn't locked in either, and regional roster rules might force the org's hand regardless of who they'd rather keep.

## Where I Land

I spent most of this year arguing there was still talent worth believing in here. Turns out Sentinels reached the same conclusion, just from the front office side instead of the couch. Jerrwin is the first domino. He won't be the last one to fall before 2027 kicks off.
