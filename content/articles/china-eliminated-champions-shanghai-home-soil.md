All four VCT China teams are out of Champions in Shanghai. Not one of them won a series on home soil.

## How It Ended

Day 8 finished the job. Global Esports swept EDward Gaming 13-5 on Summit and 13-6 on Sunset, with UdoTan opening up a 7-0 lead on the first map. Then T1 beat JDG 2-1: Lotus 13-11 to T1, Abyss 13-3 to JDG, and Haven 13-5 to T1.

Nongshim had already swept XLG, 13-11 on both maps. Close maps, no series win.

## Is It a Fluke?

Two readings. One is that this is variance. Maps decided by a handful of rounds can go anywhere, and Lotus at 13-11 is a coin flip. The other is that China's regional play isn't preparing teams for an event this deep.

I'm taking the second one, with a caveat. JDG's Abyss was dominant, a 13-3 that looked like the team everyone expected. Then they lost the decider by eight rounds. That's not bad luck. That's a team that couldn't reset after a win.

## What It Means

Home crowds are supposed to be an advantage. They also add pressure. Every Chinese team played good rounds in Shanghai, and none of them stacked enough of them. EDG had one player, ZmjjKK, finish with a positive KDA against GE.

The crowd got a lot of Valorant. It just didn't get a win.
