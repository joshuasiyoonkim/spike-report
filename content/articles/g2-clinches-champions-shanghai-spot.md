I picked G2 to lose in the lower bracket. I wrote it in plain text ten days ago: "I don't think either team makes it out." They just made it out, and now they're going to Shanghai.

## The Series That Proved Me Wrong

LEVIATÁN took Split 13-10, the kind of map that makes you think the rest of the series goes the same way. G2 didn't let it. Abyss went their way 13-9, and Summit closed it out 13-8, a series that tightened with every map instead of running away in either direction. That's a 2-1 that reads like a grind, not a blowout, exactly the kind of series a team that survived play-ins is supposed to lose. G2 didn't lose it. The win puts them over the points threshold for a direct Champions Shanghai berth, no play-in run required this time.

## MIBR's Stage 2 Ends the Same Day

LOUD closed out the round too, sweeping MIBR 2-0 to end their playoff run. MIBR looked live as recently as Stage 2 group play. Getting swept by a team that had dropped out of the upper bracket a day earlier is a rough way to close a season.

## Where I Land

Being wrong about a bracket call isn't the worst thing that can happen to me in this job. Being wrong about G2 specifically, after writing them off in the same paragraph as EG, is the kind you sit with for a minute. São Paulo is already the first Americas Finals held outside LA. Now it's also the site of a team punching a Champions ticket nobody outside their locker room saw coming. Good for them. Bad for my bracket.
