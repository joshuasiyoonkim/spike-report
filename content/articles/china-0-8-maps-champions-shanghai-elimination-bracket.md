China's four teams played eight maps at Champions Shanghai. They won zero. That's the verdict, and it's ugly.

## Is It a Bad Week or a Real Gap?

Bad week is the easy read. Four teams, four different opponents, one bad draw each. I don't buy it. TYLOO tied the half against G2 on Lotus and lost 13-6. On Sunset it was 13-3. Xi Lai got the same 13-3 on Sunset against Karmine Corp. That isn't variance, that's a team getting figured out in a half.

## JDG Was the One That Hurt

JD Gaming were the last unbeaten hope going into Day 4. On Summit they led FUT 8-4 at the half and still lost 13-10. Up four, on home soil, and it slipped. Ascent was 13-1 for FUT before that, so the whole series was one good half of Summit and nothing else.

## What Happens Next

All four are in win-or-go-home matches now. TYLOO play Team Liquid, Xi Lai play Nongshim RedForce, EDG play Global Esports, and JDG play T1. Every one of those is a coin flip on paper, and each pairing guarantees a loser. Global and T1 lost their openers too, so JDG and EDG at least get opponents who are just as shaky.

I think at least two Chinese teams survive. I could be wrong.

But if the four of them go 0-4 again, it stops being a bad week. It's a scoreboard.
