Today's the match I've been waiting on since Friday. NRG and 100 Thieves play the Upper Final of VCT Americas Stage 2 in São Paulo, the first time these Finals have ever left LA, and the winner walks straight into Sunday's grand final. The loser drops to Saturday's lower final and has to win two more series just to get back here.

## Same Two Teams, Different Stakes

This isn't the first time this year these two played for something big. 100 Thieves beat NRG 3-1 in the EWC grand final back in July, closing it out in overtime on Ascent after NRG built a 9-3 lead in the deciding map and still lost it. Cryocells took Finals MVP that day with a series-high 212 ACS, and NRG left Paris as reigning World Champions without a trophy for the third straight international event. That's the version of NRG I keep picturing today.

## What Got Them Here

100 Thieves didn't blink getting to São Paulo, sweeping LEVIATÁN 2-0 in the semis with Timoteo closing out the deciding map on his own. NRG needed the long way against LOUD, down 7-5 at half on Ascent before storming back to take it 13-7, dropping Abyss, then shutting the door on Lotus 13-5 to win the series 2-1.

## Where I Land

I picked NRG last week and I'm not backing off it, but I'm picking it for a specific reason. They've already lost this exact matchup once this year with a trophy on the line, and that kind of loss either breaks a team or sharpens one. Everything about how they closed out LOUD says sharpens, not breaks. I'll know by tonight if I'm wrong twice in a row.
