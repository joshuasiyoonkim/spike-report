FNS apologized for Masterclass not practicing, and it's the right call. The stream team went 0-2 in the NA Kickoff qualifier Swiss stage and was out before the double-elimination bracket even started.

## How did it get here?

Masterclass looked unstoppable on the way in. s0m, Subroza, yay, Marved and florescent, with FNS coaching, won all seven series in Open Qualifier #1 without dropping a map. Then the real teams showed up. FlyQuest came back from a 13-10 Haven loss to take Corrode and Bind 13-9, 13-9. counterfeit followed with a 2-0. Two losses, out.

Before the qualifier, FNS said that if they won with no practice, every org team they beat should be dropped. Bold. It didn't age well.

## Is the apology real?

That's the question chat asked, and the clip up top answers it. He says he gets it now: "I'm sorry that we didn't do everything in our power to win in terms of practicing." He even floats streaming scrims next time.

You could read it as damage control. The stream team pitch was always fun first, and fans who bought in for results maybe misread what they were watching. That's a fair reading.

I don't buy it. Once you sign up for a VCT qualifier, you're taking a slot a team that grinds scrims every night wanted. I've had a five stack that thought vibes would carry us through a Premier playoff. We got rolled by a team that clearly had a set on every map, and nobody in our call was laughing after.

## What it means

FlyQuest and counterfeit proved the gap between open qualifier talent and teams that prep is still real. That's good for NA.

FNS said they got "first rounded" and it was humbling. Good. Practice next time.
