100 Thieves beat FUT 2-0 to lock up a Champions playoff spot. Split went 13-5, Lotus 13-10. On the scoreboard, that's a clean win. The series itself wasn't clean.

## Four Pauses on One Map

Split had four technical pauses. The first came after round one, another after round two. Then xeus pulled the trigger with a Spectre and 30 bullets in the mag, in a 2v1, and nothing came out. Pause. No rollback.

Is that a bug that decides a round? In a 2v1 with a full magazine, I think so. I'm not going to say FUT wins that map with a rollback, because Split ended 13-5 and that's a lot of rounds. But the rule shouldn't depend on whether the result would have changed.

## The Meeting Made It Worse

FUT say they had a compliance meeting a week earlier where officials told them a rollback could be considered in exactly this kind of situation. Then it happened, and the answer was no. That's the part I'd be angry about too. A hard rule is fine. A rule that changes between the meeting and the match isn't.

## Where I Land

The other reading is that Riot is protecting the integrity of a live match and can't rewind every hiccup. I get it. But this wasn't the first tech problem at this event, and 100T still had to win the maps, which they did.

They're through. FUT are left angry, and I can't say they're wrong.
