LOUD are in the grand final, and they got there by fixing exactly what broke the first time.

The video up top is the group stage meeting. Vitality won it 2-0, Ascent 13-7 and Summit 19-17 in overtime. Keep Summit in your head.

## Lotus was Vitality's, then it wasn't

Vitality picked Lotus, opened with four straight rounds and won it 13-8. At that point I figured we were getting the same series again. Experienced team, young team, same result.

Then Summit. LOUD took it 13-5. A map that went to 19-17 two weeks ago turned into a blowout, and lukxo went 26/12/4 with a 1.89 rating. Same map, same opponent, completely different team. That's the adjustment, and it's the whole series.

## What about the tech pause?

Summit had a long one, and it got rolled back this time, unlike the FUT situation earlier in the event. I'm not going to hang a 13-5 on a pause. LOUD didn't win that map because of a delay. They won it by 8 rounds.

Haven was the decider and LOUD closed it 13-11. Derke and Sayonara each finished with nine first kills across the series, so Vitality did their part. It still wasn't enough.

## Where this leaves us

LOUD are the first org to reach two Champions grand finals, and the youngest roster to get this far. Jamppi's 19/12/9 on Lotus was good, but one map doesn't make a series.

Vitality drop to the lower final, guaranteed at least third. The grand final is October 18, best of five.

I'm not betting against them on Sunday.
