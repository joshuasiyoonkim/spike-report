LOUD are the first organization to win Champions twice. 3-1 over Vitality in Shanghai, a million dollars, and the biggest redemption arc of the year.

## The Year Nobody Gave Them

The pre-final film says it plainly: a very tough year for Brazil, the lowest performance from the region in Americas history. One LOUD player says people were hate watching them and hoping they'd fail. Another calls it the hardest year of their career.

That's what this team carried into Shanghai. Not many people had them here.

## The Series

Vitality had already beaten LOUD in the group stage, and they came back through the lower bracket to get this rematch. LOUD had to take them down twice in one tournament, and they did. Summit went LOUD 13-11. Sunset was 13-5. Vitality grabbed Lotus and made it a real series. erde got the MVP nod. In the pre-final film, one LOUD player says he told a teammate it's really easy to win a championship. Funny line. He backed it up.

## Was It Really a Surprise?

Two readings here. One, LOUD got hot at the right time. Two, they were always this good and the regular season just hid it. I'm going with two. You don't beat Vitality in an upper final and a grand final on a hot streak.

The hate watchers got their answer.
