Gen.G just built the most interesting Pacific roster that isn't named T1. I like it. I'm not sure it works yet.

## The Four

Gen.G announced MaKo and Dambi on November 6. They join Karon and t3xture, who were already confirmed. That's four names. The fifth player is on trial, and t3xture said on stream that he'll be revealed later. Nobody has said who's leading the team in game either.

That second part is the real question. Four strong players with no announced IGL is a roster on paper. It's not a team yet.

## Why Dambi Matters

Dambi is the one I'd build around. The video up top is the Masters Santiago run, and it's worth the watch. He's also the guy who pulled off a 1v4 clutch against Gen.G in VCT, so this is a team signing the player who personally embarrassed them. That's a good sign for how they scout.

## Is It a Contender?

Two readings. Either this is a young, loose, high-ceiling lineup that peaks by Masters, or it's four stars waiting on a fifth piece and a shot-caller. I'm leaning toward the second one. Not because the talent isn't there. Because a trial player in November means the roster gets figured out in public.

Kickoff starts January 11, so they've got about two months to work it out.

I'd watch who they pick for that last spot. That's the whole season.
