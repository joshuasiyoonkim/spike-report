The new assist rules are correct. Shipping them without a line in the patch notes is not.

## What the rules actually are

A damage assist now needs more than 10 damage within 3 seconds of your teammate's kill. Deal 50 or more and it counts whenever you did it, same as before. Healing assists are the stricter half: the player you healed has to get a kill within 10 seconds of the heal effect ending, not within 10 seconds of you casting it.

All of that went live in 13.08. None of it was in the 13.08 notes. The 13.10 notes say so themselves, in one sentence, under Gameplay Systems.

## Why the scoreboard felt off

I play Raze, Jett and Hush, so the damage half is the one I feel. Entry is a job where you do most of your damage and then die for it. The 50 rule still covers me: tag someone for 60 on the way in, my teammate finishes them a round-length later, I get the assist.

It's the chip that disappeared. A Paint Shells fragment for 20, a Hush pull that softens someone up, the Jett knife that misses the kill. Any of that outside a 3 second window used to count. Now it doesn't.

Is that wrong? No. Assist inflation was real, and a scoreboard where 11 damage from across the map reads the same as a 90 damage body shot is a scoreboard nobody trusts. Tightening it is the right call.

The problem is the two patches in between. Players watched their assist count drop with no way to know the rule had moved, so they blamed their aim, their team, or their rank. Maybe it was an oversight rather than a decision. It still cost people two patch cycles of wondering what they were doing wrong.

Write it down next time.
